SanDisk 2025–2026 NAND & SSD Platform Matrix

Filter by media generation, enterprise/AI drives, or client Gen5 platforms. Attribution badges distinguish vendor disclosures from independent reviews.

SanDisk Platform Status (Late 2026) Key Architectural Specs & Attribution Validation & Performance Implications
BiCS8 (TLC & 2 Tb QLC)
JV Tech Brief		 Mass production backbone 218 layers, CBA wafer bonding; KIOXIA/SanDisk brief reports up to 3.6 Gb/s I/O, >23 Gb/mm², up to 42 μs read latency (1 Tb), and 8 TB per 32-die package (2 Tb QLC). Baseline media across SN861, UltraQLC, and SN8100; validate multi-plane parallelism and sustained thermal behavior.
BiCS9 (2 Tb QLC)
Vendor-Reported		 Unveiled Aug 2026 Mature BiCS8 array bonded to BiCS10-like CMOS; 4.8 Gb/s I/O; SanDisk reports +150% write BW, +75% read BW, and +85%/+40% write/read power efficiency vs. BiCS8 2 Tb QLC. Stresses higher NAND channel concurrency and tighter QLC power-efficiency envelopes without added layer-stack risk.
BiCS10 (1 Tb TLC & 2 Tb QLC)
Vendor-Reported		 1 Tb TLC sampling Jul 2026; 2 Tb QLC disclosed Aug 2026 332 layers, CBA, up to 4.8 Gb/s Toggle DDR6.0, SCA protocol, PI-LTT low-voltage I/O; SanDisk reports TLC +59% density & −10%/−34% I/O power, and QLC +60% density & +100% R/W BW vs. BiCS8. Drives PCIe 6.0 controller saturation and stricter active/I/O power validation budgets.
SN861 (Enterprise Gen5 TLC)
OCP Inspired		 Shipping PCIe Gen5 NVMe, capacities up to 16 TB; targets AI training, prompting, HPC, and mixed enterprise workloads. Full OCP compliance, low-latency QoS, and AI data-cycle workload validation.
SN670 (122.88–128 TB) & UltraQLC (256 TB) (“Stargate”)
Vendor Projections		 Shipping for revenue Q4 FY26 BiCS8 2 Tb QLC CBA, scalable multi-core controller, Direct Write QLC (no SLC buffer; first-pass PLP-safe), Dynamic Frequency Scaling (DFS, projected +10% perf at given power), projected −33% DR recycles vs. SN655. Preconditioning 128–256 TB drives demands huge wall-clock time; requires optimized steady-state workflows, first-pass QLC PLP verification, and DFS power sweeps.
WD_BLACK SN8100 / Optimus GX Pro
Independently Corroborated		 Shipping M.2 2280 PCIe 5.0 x4, SM2508 8-ch controller, 218L BiCS8 TLC, 14.9 GB/s read, 14.0 GB/s write, 2.3M/2.4M IOPS (corroborated in 3rd-party reviews), 6.5–7.0W active / 5 mW idle, nCache 4.0. Validates dynamic SLC cache exhaustion/recovery curves, DirectStorage GDeflate, low-QD responsiveness, and aggressive low-power transitions.
