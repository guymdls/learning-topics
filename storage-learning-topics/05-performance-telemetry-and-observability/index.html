The 30-Second Mental Model

— How bad benchmarks fool even smart engineers
The Core Problem

It is shockingly easy to measure fake SSD numbers: testing an empty drive inflates speed by 3×, closed-loop tools hide multi-second stalls, and fixed 5% CI alerts spam everyone with false alarms.

Plain-English Analogy

Benchmarking a brand-new SSD is like timing a sprinter for 10 meters and claiming that's their marathon pace. You must warm the drive into Steady State first!

Why You Care at SanDisk

Hyperscale customers judge SanDisk drives on steady-state p99.99 tail latency and clean OCP C0h–C9h telemetry logs — not peak empty-drive numbers.

Three Observability Superpowers

— Plain-English profiling, honest latency & log decoding
1. Host Profiling

Flame Graphs & eBPF

Check Utilization, Saturation, Errors (USE) first. Capture a CPU / Off-CPU Flame Graph with perf and a live NVMe latency histogram with bpftrace — zero guesswork about whether Linux or the SSD was slow.

2. Latency Math

Coordinated Omission & Otava

Think of a coffee shop line: if the cashier freezes for 5 minutes, 50 arriving customers suffer, not just the 1 person at the counter! Pace I/Os on a timer, never average percentiles, and use Apache Otava change-point detection in nightly CI.

3. SSD Black-Box Logs

NVMe, OCP & nvme sndk

Standard 02h gives basic SMART; 07h/08h dump firmware telemetry; OCP C0h tracks physical flash writes, ECC retries & PLP capacitor health; OCP C9h translates binary telemetry strings without secret vendor tools!
