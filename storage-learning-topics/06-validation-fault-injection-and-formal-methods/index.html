The 30-Second Mental Model

— How validation engineers think differently from app developers
The Core Problem

Any drive looks great when reading sequential files at room temperature. Customer disasters happen when power flickers mid-write, a controller core hangs during reset, or a malformed command overflows a parser.

Plain-English Analogy

Think of yourself as a crash-test engineer for data: you yank the power cord in microseconds (Quarch), trigger fake engine fires (OCP C0h), and check every single byte's fingerprint (T10 CRC) afterward.

Why You Care at SanDisk

SanDisk combines big lab iron (OakGate, SANBlaze, Quarch) with modern software superpowers: QEMU shift-left emulation, smart fuzzing, and TLA+ mathematical proofs.

Know the Lab Hardware + Shift-Left Workflow

— Plain-English guide to commercial rigs & governance
Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Racks

Who Does What in the Lab?

  • OakGate (Teledyne LeCroy) & SANBlaze: Turnkey SSD test appliances running 450+ automated OCP 2.6, NVMe, and SPDM compliance tests (including Gen6 R300-G6-PRO).
  • Teledyne LeCroy Summit: Wire-level PCIe protocol analyzer (“Wireshark for physical PCIe lanes”).
  • Quarch PPM / PAM: Programmable power switches that inject microsecond brownouts, glitches & hot-plugs.
  • UNH-IOL & Windows HLK: Industry NVMe conformance & Microsoft WHQL certification suites.
Fast Iteration

Shift Left on QEMU + Safe AI Usage

Don’t wait in line for a Gen6 rack to test your Python log parser! Boot a local QEMU VM with virtual NVMe (ocp=on, fdp=on) and mock tests first:

-device nvme-subsys,id=sub0,fdp=on,fdp.nruh=4 -device nvme,id=nvme0,subsys=sub0,ocp=on

AI Governance: Approved AI coding/log assistants are encouraged — just verify every generated test against specs/mocks and never paste confidential FW dumps into unapproved external tools.
