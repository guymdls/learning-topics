The Core Problem

Studying 22+ storage topics as a flat list of acronyms feels overwhelming — and makes it impossible to tell whether a slow test is caused by your Python script, Linux locks, a bad PCIe riser cable, or NAND flash garbage collection.

Plain-English Analogy

Think of an SSD test bench like a 5-story building (App → OS → PCIe Wire → SSD Controller → Flash Cells) with security cameras (Telemetry) and a fire-drill panel (Fault Injection) wired into every floor.

Why You Care at SanDisk

Your job has two halves: (1) prove SanDisk drives stay safe under brutal power/error stress, and (2) make your host test tools fast enough for 28 GB/s & 5.5–6.8M IOPS Gen6 SSDs.

Active SanDisk Spec Baseline vs. Late-2026 Roadmap
  • Active Team Baseline: NVMe Base 2.3, NVM Command Set 1.2, PCIe Transport 1.3, TCG Storage Architecture Core 2.01, TCG Opal 2.30, and OCP Datacenter NVMe SSD 2.6.
  • Late-2026 Transition: NVMe 2.4 (ratified August 2026), OCP 2.7, PCIe 6.0 enterprise SSDs (vendor-reported up to 28 GB/s & 5.5–6.8M IOPS), 332-layer BiCS10 NAND, and High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) for AI inference.
Eight High-Impact Validation Domains Added to Your Original 22 Topics
  1. Splitting “TCP” into TCG Security & NVMe/TCP Fabrics: Separates TCG Core 2.01 / Opal 2.30 encryption from NVMe/TCP (1.2/1.3) & RoCEv2 network storage.
  2. Validator-as-a-System Engineering: Eliminates host CPU bottlenecks at Gen6 rates using Intel ISA-L SIMD CRCs, lock-free queues, NUMA pinning & zero-copy DMA.
  3. Out-of-Band Sidebands: NVMe-MI 2.1/2.2 over MCTP, SMBus/I2C, I3C & PCIe VDM for health polling, sideband FW updates & SPDM auth.
  4. Formal State-Machine Modeling (TLA+ / PlusCal): Mathematically verifies concurrent resets, queue invariants & PLP safety (actively recruited at SanDisk).
  5. Statistical Rigor & CI Change-Point Detection: SNIA SSS PTS steady-state math, Coordinated Omission prevention, p99.99/max & Apache Otava (Hunter).
  6. Form Factors & Signal Integrity: EDSFF E1.S/E1.L/E3.S/E3.L (SFF-TA-1002), Gen5/Gen6 riser loss & PCIe eye diagnostics (nvmeadm sandisk/pci-eye).
  7. Commercial HIL & Shift-Left Emulation: OakGate, SANBlaze, Quarch PPM/PAM, LeCroy Summit + early offline dev on QEMU/FEMU.
  8. AI-Assisted Engineering Governance: Accelerating scripting/log triage with approved AI tools while verifying outputs & protecting IP boundaries.
